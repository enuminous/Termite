# Limitations and Falsification

TERMITE is an experimental multi-agent emergence framework.

## Limitations

- Emergence depends heavily on representation.
- Network topology may be incompletely observed.
- Cluster definitions can be arbitrary.
- Simple local rules may not capture real agents.
- High coherence can represent harmful conformity.
- Collective novelty is not automatically useful.

## Falsification criteria

TERMITE's central hypothesis is weakened if:

1. Standard network metrics explain outcomes equally well.
2. Removing topology has little effect.
3. Independent-agent baselines match collective behavior.
4. Perturbation amplification is not reproducible.
5. Attractor structure does not generalize across seeds.
6. Emergence scores are unstable under reasonable representations.

## Claim boundary

TERMITE measures collective behavior under a specified multi-agent model. It does not establish consciousness, agency, or intelligence of the collective.
